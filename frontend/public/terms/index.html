Ashkey Terms

Short, because there is not much we can promise or see. Interactive site: mod6m-qiaaa-aaaac-bgkwq-cai.icp0.io

What you get. Encrypted storage space and/or heritage switches for the period you paid for, provided by smart contracts running on the Internet Computer. No warranty: software has bugs and networks have outages. Keep your own copies of anything irreplaceable.

Your account number is your only key. It is never sent to us and cannot be recovered. Losing it means losing access to everything on the account.

No KYC. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or any identity document, now or later. An account is just a random number, and the service has nowhere to store personal details.

Payments. Prices are set in US dollars per year and paid in XMR (Monero only): the dollar price is converted at the current rate when you order, and that XMR amount is guaranteed for 24 hours (after that it is recalculated). Payments are final: no refunds, and no account recovery. Send the exact amount to the address shown for your order; amounts sent elsewhere cannot be matched to your account.

Expiry. Vault files are deleted automatically a few days after the paid period ends. Unrenewed heritage switches release to their heirs after a grace period, and released switches are deleted 180 days later.

Your responsibility. You may not use Ashkey to store or share anything illegal where you live or under the laws that apply to the service. We cannot see your content, but that does not make illegal use legal.

Changes. The code is open source. While the canisters still have a controller, the service may be updated; the trust page shows the live code hash and controllers at any time.
